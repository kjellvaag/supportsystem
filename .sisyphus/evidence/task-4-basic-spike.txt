Task T4: Spike — Basic-injeksjon (proof of concept)
Dato: 2026-09-07

MÅL
Bevise at en LibreOffice Basic-makro kan injiseres i en openpyxl-generert
.xlsx som er konvertert til .ods, og at .ods-filen fortsatt er gyldig.

MILJØ
- Python 3.11.9, openpyxl 3.1.5
- LibreOffice installert på C:\Program Files\LibreOffice\program\soffice.exe
- Arbeidsmappe: C:\Users\kjell\AppData\Local\Temp\opencode\t4-spike

STEG 1: Lag minimal .xlsx
-------------------------
from openpyxl import Workbook
wb = Workbook(); ws = wb.active; ws.title = "Demo"
ws["A1"] = "hello"; ws["A2"] = "world"
wb.save("min.xlsx")

Resultat: min.xlsx (4839 bytes)

STEG 2: Konverter til .ods med soffice
---------------------------------------
& "C:\Program Files\LibreOffice\program\soffice.exe" `
  --headless --convert-to ods --outdir . min.xlsx

Resultat: min.ods (8987 bytes)

STEG 3: Inspiser ren .ods
-------------------------
zip-namelist (relevant):
  mimetype
  manifest.rdf
  Configurations2/
  styles.xml
  settings.xml
  meta.xml
  Thumbnails/thumbnail.png
  content.xml
  META-INF/manifest.xml

Ingen Basic/-mappe finnes i en ren soffice-konvertert .ods.

STEG 4: Injiser Basic-XML
-------------------------
Lagt til i .ods-zipen:
  Basic/script-lc.xml
  Basic/Standard/script-lb.xml
  Basic/Standard/Module1.xml

Module1.xml inneholder:
  Sub Hello()
      MsgBox "hei"
  End Sub

Patchet META-INF/manifest.xml med:
  <manifest:file-entry manifest:full-path="Basic/" manifest:media-type="application/binary"/>
  <manifest:file-entry manifest:full-path="Basic/script-lc.xml" manifest:media-type="text/xml"/>
  <manifest:file-entry manifest:full-path="Basic/Standard/" manifest:media-type="application/binary"/>
  <manifest:file-entry manifest:full-path="Basic/Standard/script-lb.xml" manifest:media-type="text/xml"/>
  <manifest:file-entry manifest:full-path="Basic/Standard/Module1.xml" manifest:media-type="text/xml"/>

Resultat: injected.ods (9415 bytes)

STEG 5: Verifiser med soffice round-trip .ods -> .xlsx
-------------------------------------------------------
& "C:\Program Files\LibreOffice\program\soffice.exe" `
  --headless --convert-to xlsx --outdir roundtrip injected.ods

Resultat: roundtrip/injected.xlsx (5896 bytes)
openpyxl bekrefter:
  sheets: ['Demo']
  A1 = 'hello'

Konklusjon: LibreOffice aksepterer den injiserte .ods-filen uten reparasjon.

STEG 6: Verifiser at makroen overlever ODS -> ODS
--------------------------------------------------
& "C:\Program Files\LibreOffice\program\soffice.exe" `
  --headless --convert-to ods --outdir ods2ods injected.ods

Etter konvertering finnes fortsatt:
  Basic/Standard/Module1.xml
  Basic/Standard/script-lb.xml
  Basic/script-lc.xml

Module1.xml er byte-identisk med den injiserte kilden.

STEG 7: Test av gjenbrukbart injektor-skript
---------------------------------------------
python .sisyphus/evidence/spike/basic/injiser_basic.py `
  .sisyphus/evidence/spike/basic/artifacts/min.ods `
  .sisyphus/evidence/spike/basic/artifacts/test_injected.ods --verify

Output:
  Injisert Module1 i .sisyphus\evidence\spike\basic\artifacts\test_injected.ods
  soffice round-trip OK: ark = ['Demo']

Viktig lærdom
-------------
META-INF/manifest.xml MÅ patches med file-entry for hver Basic-del.
Uten manifest-patching produserer soffice INGEN output ved
headless-konvertering (tomt output-dir).
